Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Kazy Video Uploader ("the App") is a personal tool used only by its developer to upload videos to the developer's own YouTube channel. The App uses YouTube API Services.

YouTube API Services

By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The App's use of data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the App accesses

  • An OAuth access token for the developer's own Google account, granted through Google's consent screen.
  • The list of videos on the developer's own YouTube channel (titles, upload status, scheduled publish times), used to avoid duplicate uploads and to schedule new videos.

The App does not access any other users' data.

How information is used

The information is used only to upload videos, set their metadata and thumbnails, and schedule their publication on the developer's own channel.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the developer's own computer. The App has no server, and no data is sent anywhere other than Google's APIs.

Sharing

The App does not sell, share, or disclose any information to third parties.

Revoking access

Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Deleting the locally stored token also removes the App's access on that computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
